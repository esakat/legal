8. Ads and unlocking the special angles

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. There are two kinds of ad:

  • Long ad (rewarded ad, optional): a video ad that you choose to watch yourself. If you watch it to the end, then for the rest of that day (until the date changes on your device) the special angles (high, low, and foreshortened views) are available and the short ad before practice is no longer shown
  • Short ad (interstitial ad): a full-screen ad shown once at the start of each practice session, if you have not watched a long ad to the end that day. However many poses you draw in one session, it is shown only that one time at the start

If you have bought "Remove ads" (section 7), neither kind of ad is shown. No practice feature is itself restricted by whether you watch ads.

The special angles (high, low, and foreshortened views) are available in either of these cases:

  • You have bought "Remove ads" — always available
  • You watched a long ad to the end that day — available for the rest of that day (until the date changes on your device)

If you close the long ad early, or no ad could be shown, the special angles are not unlocked and the short ad continues to be shown. Ads may be unavailable depending on your connection, ad inventory, or your advertising consent status (for users in the UK and other regions where consent is required), and the Developer does not guarantee that unlocking by long ad is possible at all times. The effect of a long ad applies only to the device on which you watched it. When a short ad cannot be shown, the session starts without one.

Locked special angles are not served in sessions.
