Privacy Policy

Effective: August 29, 2026

Gesdro (the "App") is a gesture drawing practice app developed and provided by esaka (an individual developer, the "Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. No personal information is collected

The App does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal information. There is no account or sign-in.

2. Your drawings and practice records stay on your device

Your sketches, practice records (dates, counts, streaks), imported photos, pose sets, and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the Developer's servers or to any third party. Nobody but you can access them. Deleting the App also deletes this data.

3. Advertising

To keep the App free, it displays ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). An ad is shown at most once per day, just before you begin a practice session.

For ad delivery and measurement, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see section 4)
  • Coarse region, device type, and OS version
  • Records of ad impressions and interactions

Google's policies apply to this use. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The App restricts ad content ratings so that violent or sexual advertising is not served.

4. App Tracking Transparency

The App may ask for permission to track in order to personalize ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", every feature of the App continues to work exactly the same. You will simply see ads that are not based on your interests. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

5. Information that is never collected

  • Contact details such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, calendars, your full photo library, or health data
  • The contents of your sketches

Photo import uses the standard iOS photo picker, so only the photos you select are passed to the App. The App never accesses your entire photo library.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. About the included photos

The pose photos bundled with the App were created by contributors to Wikimedia Commons and are licensed under CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA. Author, license, and source are credited for every photo in the App's Credits screen. Photos that were cropped to center the figure are marked as such.

If you are pictured in one of these photos and would like it removed, please contact us using the address below and we will handle your request individually.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete individual poses from the Gallery, or uninstall the App to remove everything. Because the data exists only on your device, these actions delete it completely. There is no data on the Developer's side to request deletion of.

9. Changes to this policy

If the handling of information changes — for example when a paid option to hide ads is added — this policy will be revised. Revisions are posted on this page with the date, and significant changes are also announced in the App.
