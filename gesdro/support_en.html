Frequently asked questions

Why can I only draw with Apple Pencil?

Finger drawing is disabled so that the hand resting on the screen never draws by mistake.

Where are my drawings stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them too, so use the share option in the enlarged sketch view to export anything you want to keep.

Can I remove the ads?

One ad is shown per day, just before you start practicing. No features are locked behind it — the free version is the full app. A one-time purchase to hide ads is planned for a future update.

I was asked to allow tracking

That prompt only controls whether ads are personalized. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" keeps every feature working. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Can I practice with my own photos?

Yes. Import them from the Gallery via "From Photos" or "From Files". They stay on your device.

I want to practice the same subjects every day

In the Gallery, tap "Select", choose your poses, then "Add to Set". Pick that set under "Source" in the session settings, and your choice is remembered for next time.

Who owns the pose photos?

They were created by contributors to Wikimedia Commons under CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA. Author, license, and source are credited for every photo in the app's Credits screen.

I appear in one of the photos and want it removed

Please email us with the work's title (shown in the Credits screen) or a screenshot. We will confirm and remove it in the next update.
