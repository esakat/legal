Frequently asked questions

I can't draw with my finger. Is Apple Pencil required?

Yes, drawing requires an Apple Pencil. Finger drawing is disabled so that the hand resting on the screen never draws by mistake. Buttons and menus still work with your finger. The app is for iPad only.

Where are my drawings stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them too, so use the share option in the enlarged sketch view to export anything you want to keep.

When are ads shown?

There are two kinds of ad.

  • Long ad (optional): a video ad that you choose to watch yourself. If you watch it to the end, then for the rest of that day (until the date changes on your device) the special angles (high, low, and foreshortened views) are available and the short ad before practice is no longer shown
  • Short ad: shown once at the start of each practice session, if you have not watched a long ad to the end that day. However many poses you draw in one session, it is shown only that one time at the start

Ads never interrupt a session and are never shown on the results screen. If you buy "Remove ads", neither kind of ad is shown.

Can I remove the ads?

Yes. Open Settings › Remove ads in the app to buy the one-time option. A single purchase removes both the long and the short ads and keeps the special angles (high, low, and foreshortened views) always available. It is not a subscription. The price is shown in the Settings screen.

Without the purchase, a short ad is shown once at the start of each practice session (unless you have watched a long ad to the end that day). The practice features — duration, count, practice patterns, and history — are free and unrestricted.

The "Remove ads" price shows "Preparing" and I can't buy it

That appears when the app could not get the product information from the App Store. Check your connection, then reopen the Settings screen or try again later. If it persists, please contact us at the address above.

How do I restore my purchase?

After a reinstall or on a new iPad, sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then tap Settings › Restore Purchases in the app. You will not be charged again. If it does not restore, check that the Apple Account signed in on the iPad is the one used for the purchase.

Can I share "Remove ads" with my family?

This purchase may be eligible for Apple's Family Sharing. Whether it is, and how it is shared, depends on Apple's system and your Apple Account settings. See Apple's support pages for how to set up Family Sharing.

Refunds

Payment and refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, and the developer cannot issue a refund directly. To request one, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Once a purchase is refunded, its benefits (no ads, and permanent access to the special angles) end.

Unlocking the special angles

The special angles (high, low, and foreshortened views) are for practicing depth and foreshortening. You can open them in either of two ways:

  • Watch a long ad to the end (an ad you choose to watch yourself, for example from "Watch an ad to unlock" in the Gallery) — they stay open for the rest of that day (until the date changes on your device). For that day, the short ad before practice is no longer shown either
  • Buy "Remove ads" — they are always available

The basic angles (front, three-quarter, side, back) are always free. You can check the current state under Settings › Special angles. Locked special angles are not served in sessions.

I watched an ad but the special angles did not unlock

They unlock only when a long ad is watched to the end; closing it early unlocks nothing, and the short ad before practice continues to be shown. Watching the short ad shown before practice does not unlock the special angles. Ads may also be unavailable depending on your connection or ad inventory — please try again later. If you see "Ads need your consent", open Settings › Change ad settings and review your choices.

How do I change my ad settings?

There are two separate settings.

  • Consent to data use for ads (users in the UK and other regions where consent is required): change or withdraw it at any time in the app under Settings › Change ad settings. This item appears only in regions where consent is required (and is hidden once you have bought "Remove ads")
  • Tracking permission (all regions): change it in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security › Tracking

See the Privacy Policy for details.

I was asked to allow tracking

That prompt only controls whether ads are personalized. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" keeps the app working exactly the same. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I switch the language?

The app supports Japanese and English. Tap Settings › Change language in the app to open the app's page in the iOS Settings app, then choose under Language. If no Language item appears, first add the language you want under iOS Settings › General › Language & Region, then try again.

Can I practice with my own photos?

Not in the current version. You practice with the pose references bundled with the app, and there is no feature for adding your own photos or images. If you would like one, please let us know at the address above.

I want to practice the same subjects every day

In this version, sessions draw from all the bundled poses. There is no feature for practicing only poses you pick. In the Gallery, tap a pose to look back at the sketches you have made of it. If you would like a way to narrow the poses down, please let us know at the contact below.

Who owns the pose references? Can I publish what I draw?

What you draw from the bundled poses is your own work. You may post it, sell it, or use it however you like, and no credit is required.

The bundled pose images are CG made by the developer using assets (body meshes, clothes, hair, and the like) published by the MakeHuman community under CC0. The app does not include photographs of real people. The source and license of the underlying assets are credited in the app under Gallery › Credits.

Copyright in the bundled pose images themselves belongs to the developer (the underlying assets are CC0; the bundled images are not). You are free to use them as references for practice inside the app, but you may not redistribute the images themselves, repost them as a reference pack or pose collection, or distribute them as a training dataset. See section 2 of the Terms of Use for details.
